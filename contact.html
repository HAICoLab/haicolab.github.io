We Are Hiring!

PhD Students

We are looking for highly motivated PhD students with strong backgrounds in computer science, mathematics, or related fields.

Master's Students

Master's positions are available for students interested in AI research and NLP applications.

Undergraduate Researchers

Undergraduate students can join as research interns to gain hands-on experience in AI research.

What We Look For:

  • Strong programming skills (Python, PyTorch/TensorFlow)
  • Solid foundation in machine learning and deep learning
  • Passion for research and problem-solving
  • Good communication skills in English and Korean
  • Self-motivated and able to work independently
How to Apply: Send your CV, transcript, and a brief statement of research interests to nlpysheo@sogang.ac.kr
