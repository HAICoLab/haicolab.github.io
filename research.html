Research

Advancing Human-AI Collaboration through Data-Centric Approaches

Research Areas

Data-centric AI
Developing methodologies and frameworks for improving AI systems through systematic data enhancement, quality assessment, and curation strategies. Focus on creating efficient pipelines for data labeling, augmentation, and validation.
Large Language Models
Investigating the capabilities, limitations, and potential applications of large language models. Research includes model evaluation, fine-tuning strategies, prompt engineering, and alignment with human preferences.
Natural Language Processing
Advancing natural language understanding and generation through novel architectures and training methodologies. Applications include dialogue systems, text generation, information extraction, and semantic analysis.
Human-AI Collaboration
Designing and evaluating systems that effectively combine human expertise with AI capabilities. Research focuses on interactive AI systems, human-in-the-loop learning, and collaborative decision-making frameworks.
