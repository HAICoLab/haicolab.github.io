About

Welcome to the Human-AI Collaboration Lab for Data-Centric Research (HAICoLab) at Sogang University. Our research focuses on advancing the synergy between humans and artificial intelligence through data-centric approaches.

We investigate how to build, evaluate, and improve AI systems that effectively collaborate with humans, with particular emphasis on natural language processing and large language models. Our work spans fundamental research in data-centric AI methodologies to practical applications in real-world scenarios.

Research Interests

  • Data-centric AI: Developing methodologies for improving AI through better data
  • Human-AI Collaboration: Designing systems for effective human-AI interaction
  • AI Literacy: Enhancing understanding and effective use of AI systems
  • Large Language Models / NLP: Advancing natural language understanding and generation
