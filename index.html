About

Bridging linguistics, the humanities, and AI

허윤석은 서강대학교 영문학부(Division of English)에서 언어와 인공지능의 접점을 연구하고 가르칩니다. 인간-AI 협업(Human-AI Collaboration), AI 리터러시, 인간 중심 AI, 자연어처리를 중심으로 연구하며, 언어학적·인문학적 통찰이 AI 시스템의 설계와 평가를 어떻게 이끌 수 있는지에 관심이 있습니다.

Before joining the Division of English, my research focused on core NLP and multimodal learning: word sense disambiguation, Korean named entity recognition, multimodal machine translation, audio-visual scene-aware dialog, scene graph reasoning, and controllable video captioning. Recent work moves toward culturally grounded reasoning in large language models, process reward models, persona-based data augmentation, and LLM-driven analysis of literary text.

In the classroom I teach students in the humanities to read, question, and build with AI — from vibe coding and data analysis to prompt engineering and benchmark construction — so that humanistic expertise becomes a source of value in the AI era.

Research Areas

Four threads that connect my past work in NLP and multimodal learning with current questions about how people and AI think together.

🤝

Human-AI Collaboration & Human-centered AI

How people and language models can collaborate productively — interactive, controllable systems designed around human needs rather than model capability alone.

Controllable GenerationInteractive Systems
🌏

Cultural Reasoning & Alignment of LLMs

Understanding when models recognize local cultural norms yet override them mid-reasoning, and building process reward models and persona-aware data to fix it.

Process Reward ModelsData Augmentation
🎬

Multimodal Language Understanding

Grounding language in vision and audio: multimodal machine translation, audio-visual scene-aware dialog, scene graph reasoning, and video captioning.

Vision-LanguageDialog
📚

AI Literacy & AI for the Humanities

Data-centric AI education for humanities students, and LLM-based analysis of literary text — for example narrative function transitions in Hemingway.

AI EducationComputational Literary Studies
